## The Growing Energy Challenge for Indian Industries

Walk the floor of any factory in Haryana, Gujarat, or Maharashtra, and you'll find a common pain point: the power bill. For most manufacturers, energy is the second or third largest operating expense — and unlike raw materials, it's rarely optimised.

Beyond cost, there's a bigger pressure building. ESG mandates, investor expectations, and global supply-chain sustainability requirements are forcing Indian industries to rethink where their electricity comes from. Companies targeting RE100 — 100% renewable energy — need more than good intentions. They need actionable pathways. And they need a partner who understands the operational realities of large-scale industrial facilities.

Businesses can no longer afford to treat energy as just another utility bill. It has become a competitive advantage.

## Our Industrial Energy Solutions

- **Industrial Solar** — We design, engineer, procure, install, commission, and maintain solar power plants for factories, warehouses, and production facilities. Every project includes civil & structural works, mechanical & electrical installation, safety documentation, and testing.
- **Open Access** — We enable large-scale consumers to directly procure renewable energy from third-party generators using the existing grid infrastructure, unlocking energy cost savings that were previously inaccessible to mid-size industry.
- **Group Captive** — A regulatory structure allowing multiple businesses to jointly own a renewable energy plant, sharing both investment and output.
- **CAPEX** — A model that allows businesses to own their solar power system through an upfront investment, delivering maximum long-term savings, asset ownership, and greater energy independence.
- **OPEX** — A model that enables businesses to adopt solar with **zero upfront investment**. Customers pay only for the electricity generated, while the provider owns and maintains the system under a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA).
- **RenewX Training** — Our dedicated training vertical bridges the skill gap in renewable energy through subscription-based training modules and AI-driven platforms, empowering the next generation of clean energy professionals across India.
